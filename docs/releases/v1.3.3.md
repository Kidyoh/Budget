Event Budget is an event planning and budget app for Android and iOS: budgets, expenses with instalment payments, tasks, notes, guests and vendors, in a lime-and-ink design with light and dark themes. All data stays on your phone.

## What's in this version

- **Design:** an electric lime and near-black look, very rounded cards, big balances with small decimals, and light and dark themes.
- **Onboarding:** three welcome pages that ask for your name for the greeting.
- **Home:** the budget left across your events, event cards styled like bank cards, and upcoming payments across all events.
- **Event screens:** a floating navigation bar (Overview, Money, Tasks, People, Notes) with a "+" button that adds whatever fits the current section.
- **Money:** a stacked bar chart per category (paid, unpaid and unspent budget), and expenses with instalment payments, receipt photos and due dates.
- **Birr:** Ethiopian Birr is the default currency, and the app uses its own Birr icon: the Ethiopic letter ብ with currency bars.
- **Branding:** a new app icon and splash screen.
